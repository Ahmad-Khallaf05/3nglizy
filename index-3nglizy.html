About 3nglizy

Learning English Shouldn't Feel Like an Exam — It Should Feel Like Coffee with Friends.

3nglizy was created with a warm mission: to bridge the gap between classroom theory and real-world confidence. Most people understand English, but freeze when it's time to speak. We change that by bringing enthusiastic learners together around comfortable cafe tables.

  • Safe, Non-Judgmental Space
    Make mistakes freely and laugh it off — everyone at the table is learning and supporting each other.
  • Guided Conversation Topic Cards
    No awkward silences! Our friendly table hosts bring curated topic cards, debates, and hilarious icebreaker games.
  • Great Coffee & Cozy Spots
    We partner with the best specialty coffee shops to ensure warm lighting, comfy seating, and delicious drinks.

The 3nglizy Difference

Why You'll Love Practicing With Us

We reimagined language practice to be social, engaging, and genuinely enjoyable.

Cozy Cafe Vibes

Forget fluorescent classroom lights. Enjoy warm armchairs, the aroma of fresh coffee, and relaxed ambient music.

Small Tables (Max 6)

No getting lost in a crowd. In our small circles, every participant gets 20–30+ minutes of actual talking time.

Zero Grammar Shaming

We focus on confidence and expression first. Getting your ideas across matters far more than memorizing irregular verbs.

Fun Topic Prompt Cards

Never worry about what to say next! Our custom conversation decks include intriguing dilemmas, fun quizzes, and debates.

Enthusiastic Facilitators

Our friendly table leaders keep energy high, introduce everyone, and gently assist whenever you're searching for a word.

Genuine Friendships

Meet ambitious students, creatives, and professionals who share your goals. Most of our members stay friends outside the club!
